# Turbocharged.HeatExchanger.Furnace
A "Turbo burn barrel" is a device that burns firewood at extreme pressures fed by a turbocharger inside a sealed pressure vessel like in air compressor tank. If we combine a countercurrent heat exchanger for exhaust to intake heat recovery with refractory materials and a turbocharger.

Without a turbo as the furnace heats up in the core that expanding air will be less dense and push both the intake and the exhaust air outward. The furnace can only get so hot, even with the heat exchanger. This creates pressure volume work and energy is lost pushing the atmosphere out to create a lower densitythis

The turbocharger crates a dynamic restriction at the intake and exhaust outlet pipes, creating back pressure at the exhaust and positive pressure at the intake. Thus keeping pressure inside the furnace contained, converting pressure-volume work that would otherwise be lost to atmosphere into more oxygen thus higher temperature and more rapid combustion!
